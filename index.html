Jan H. Wasserziehr

I am a political philosopher working at the intersection of critical theory and the philosophy of AI. I am especially interested in the relationship between technical progress and:

  • modern ideas of human freedom; and
  • human cognition, reason, and autonomy.

I am completing a PhD in Political Theory at the London School of Economics, supervised by Katrin Flikschuh and advised by Lea Ypi. My thesis, Fleeting Freedom: Technical Progress and the Fate of Modernity, is a critical enquiry into the ambiguities immanent to the modern project to ground human freedom in technical progress and the domination of nature.

Alongside the thesis, I work on the ethics of artificial consciousness, and in particular on the question of whether artificial systems could have moral status.

I was previously a visiting scholar at Columbia University and Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin. Before the PhD, I worked for three years at a political consultancy in Berlin.
